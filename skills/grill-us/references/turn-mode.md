# Turn mode

Use turn mode when participants share an account, terminal, device, or microphone and the harness cannot identify speakers reliably.

## Choose an attribution convention

Ask for participant names, then choose one convention:

1. Prefix each contribution with `Name:`.
2. Pass the device and announce the next speaker.
3. Let a facilitator relay an answer while naming its source.

Prefer name prefixes because they remain visible in the transcript.

## Preserve attribution

- Attribute labelled text to the named speaker.
- Ask who spoke when an unlabelled contribution could change a decision.
- Keep separately labelled contributions separate even when they arrive in one message.
- Mark paraphrased answers as relayed until the named speaker confirms them.

Address questions by name and allow answers in any order that follows the chosen convention. After each round, summarize who answered and who remains.

If the convention repeatedly fails, pause the product interview and re-establish attribution before continuing.
